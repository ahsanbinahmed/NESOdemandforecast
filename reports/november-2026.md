## Matched comparison

Across the 64 half-hours with Demand Lab forecasts, NESO MAE was 556.8 MW, the fixed-correction MAE was 487.7 MW, and Demand Lab MAE was 415.7 MW. Demand Lab's bias was +137.7 MW, versus NESO's +416.1 MW.

Demand Lab won on daily MAE on 10 of 16 scored nights. The aggregate advantage was concentrated on 12 and 14 November. Excluding those two dates, Lab MAE was 418.0 MW and NESO MAE was 407.7 MW.

November 1 and 2 have actuals and original/corrected forecasts. The documented Demand Lab model excludes those dates because its historical inputs intersect a clock change. They are unscored for Lab. The matched headline excludes them from every model.
